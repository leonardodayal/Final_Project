This project, conducted by Trenner Rathkey and Leonardo Dayal, builds a smart fantasy football trade calculator that uses machine learning to score how much a team improves from a trade. First, we will use regression models trained on past player stats to predict future performance scores for individual players. These predictions are then used to give values that are used to create an overall team strength rating. By comparing the team's rating before and after a simulated trade, our model calculates the exact point improvement and shows where the improvement is occurring to show whether a trade is fair or lopsided.
